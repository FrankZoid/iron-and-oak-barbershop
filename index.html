About Iron & Oak

Iron & Oak Barbershop was built for clients who want a reliable cut without the fuss. The shop combines old-school barbering standards with a relaxed neighborhood feel, making it easy to come in, get cleaned up, and get on with your day.

Iron & Oak Barbershop is a local Richmond barbershop focused on clean cuts, friendly service, and a comfortable experience. We serve clients looking for classic cuts, modern fades, beard trims, shaves, and kids' cuts in a straightforward neighborhood setting.
